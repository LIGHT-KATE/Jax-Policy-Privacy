# Jax-Policy-Privacy

Jax Paradise Bot — Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Jax Paradise Bot handles information when used on Discord.

1. Information We May Access

Jax Paradise Bot may have access to basic Discord information necessary for its functions, such as:

- Discord usernames, display names, and user IDs
- Server IDs and server-related information
- User roles and permissions
- Information provided directly through commands or interactions with the bot
- Message content only when necessary for a bot feature that requires it

The exact information accessible to the bot may depend on Discord's permissions, intents, and the features enabled for the application.

2. How We Use Information

Information accessed by Jax Paradise Bot is used only to:

- Provide the bot's features and commands
- Respond to user interactions
- Perform moderation or server-related functions when enabled
- Maintain the functionality and security of the bot

We do not use information accessed by the bot for advertising or selling personal information.

3. Data Storage

Jax Paradise Bot does not intentionally store personal information unless storage is necessary for a specific bot feature.

If the bot begins storing additional information in the future, this Privacy Policy will be updated to explain what information is stored and why.

4. Data Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or intentionally share users' personal information with third parties.

Information may only be disclosed when necessary to comply with applicable law or to protect the security and integrity of the bot and its users.

5. Data Retention and Deletion

If information is stored by the bot, it will only be retained for as long as reasonably necessary for the relevant feature.

Users may contact the Jax Paradise staff regarding questions about stored information or requests concerning their data.

6. Discord

Jax Paradise Bot operates through Discord and is subject to Discord's platform rules and policies.

Discord itself may collect and process information according to its own Privacy Policy. This policy applies specifically to Jax Paradise Bot and does not replace Discord's Privacy Policy.

7. Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when the bot's features or data practices change.

The "Last updated" date will be changed whenever significant changes are made.

8. Contact

For questions or concerns regarding this Privacy Policy, please contact the Jax Paradise staff through the official Jax Paradise Discord server.
